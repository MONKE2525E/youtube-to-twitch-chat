Use your Twitch login on YouTube

Already logged in on Twitch? Let the embedded chat use that same session.

Twitch storage connects automatically when the extension loads. It allows Twitch cookies only while you're on YouTube. Your password and login token stay with Twitch.

If you aren't signed in yet, open Twitch above, sign in, then return here and connect. Browser policies or privacy tools can still block embedded login; Pop out in the chat panel opens a regular Twitch chat tab.
